# Confidentiality

Everything in this handbook is **general engineering knowledge**: 3GPP KPI definitions, industry-standard procedures, ITU-R recommendations and publicly documented vendor behavior.

**Deliberately excluded:** no confidential employer data, internal network configurations, credentials, customer identifiers, or proprietary procedures appear anywhere in this repository. Content was distilled from public standards (3GPP, ITU-R, ETSI) and generic industry practice accumulated over 17 years of field work.

If anything here should be corrected or removed, please open an issue.
